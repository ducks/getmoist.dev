Moist is an independent software effort, working on developer tools, practical applications, and experimental AI.

The projects start with specific problems. How do you tell whether an agent actually fixed a server? How do you get from a pile of coffee orders to a roast plan? What can a small learning experiment tell you, and what can’t it?

Some of that work becomes software people can use. Some stays experimental. We try to make the distinction clear, document what we find, and keep the code open wherever we can.
